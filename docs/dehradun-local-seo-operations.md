# Dehradun Local SEO Operations

This runbook implements the non-code parts of the "hearing aids near me" ranking strategy for Audiosen.

## 1) Google Business Profile Setup (Day 1-3)

- Primary category: choose the single highest-revenue service category (`Hearing Aid Store` or `Audiologist`).
- Secondary categories: add only truly offered services (ENT and speech categories when available in Google list).
- Business type: confirm storefront vs hybrid.
- Service areas: list only cities/areas actually served.
- Core profile fields to complete:
  - Business name
  - Address
  - Primary phone
  - Website URL
  - Appointment/contact URL
  - Hours + holiday hours
  - Services and descriptions
  - Photos (clinic exterior, reception, testing setup, team)

## 2) Service Items To Add In GBP

- Hearing test consultation
- Pure Tone Audiometry (PTA)
- Speech audiometry
- Hearing aid fitting and programming
- Hearing aid repair and maintenance
- ENT consultation support
- Speech therapy support

Each service description should mention Dehradun context naturally and include one patient-centric outcome line.

## 3) Review Engine (Start Day 3, then daily)

Policy:
- Ask for review only after a positive patient interaction.
- Reply to every review within 24 hours.

Template 1 (WhatsApp after visit):
```
Thank you for visiting Audiosen today.
If your experience was good, please share a quick Google review.
It helps other families in Dehradun find trusted hearing and speech support.
Review link: <your Google review link>
```

Template 2 (Follow-up call/SMS):
```
Thanks again for choosing Audiosen. Your feedback helps us improve.
If you can spare 1 minute, please leave a Google review here:
<your Google review link>
```

## 5) Weekly Search Console and GA4 Routine

Search Console filters:
- Country: India
- Device: Mobile
- Queries containing: `near me`, `dehradun`, `hearing aid`, `audiologist`, `ent`, `speech therapy`

Review every week:
- Impressions
- CTR
- Average position
- Top landing pages

GA4 events to monitor:
- `click_call`
- `click_whatsapp`
- `cta_form_start`
- `contact_form_submit`
- `form_submit`
- `generate_lead`

Monthly optimization:
- Identify high-impression + low-CTR pages.
- Rewrite title and meta description.
- Improve above-the-fold copy and CTA clarity.
