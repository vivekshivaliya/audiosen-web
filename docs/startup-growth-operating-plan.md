# Audiosen startup growth operating plan

Updated: 15 August 2026

## Strategic position

Audiosen should first become the most trusted local option for assessment-led hearing-aid selection, fitting, repair, and continuing support in Dehradun. It should not compete as a general ENT hospital unless verified ENT professionals and corresponding services are genuinely available.

The market need is meaningful: India's National Programme for Prevention and Control of Deafness cites an older WHO estimate of about 63 million people with significant hearing impairment and focuses on early detection, management, and rehabilitation. Use that as evidence of the care gap, not as a current private-market revenue estimate.

The local search market is trust-heavy. Dehradun competitors promote named clinicians, large review counts, device selection, fitting, repairs, home visits, prices, warranties, and fast contact. Audiosen's defensible position should be clarity and aftercare rather than unsupported “best clinic” claims.

## Initial customer segments

1. **Older adult with gradual hearing difficulty**
   - Buyer/influencer: often an adult child aged roughly 30–55.
   - Need: respectful assessment, simple explanation, family involvement, device comparison, aftercare.
   - High-value channels: Google Maps, local search, doctor referral, family WhatsApp.

2. **Existing hearing-aid wearer with a service problem**
   - Need: cleaning, weak-sound troubleshooting, repair estimate, reprogramming, replacement guidance.
   - Strategic value: faster first transaction and an opportunity to earn long-term trust.

3. **First-time device shopper comparing price**
   - Need: a transparent written quote, what is included, brand/model differences, realistic expectations, warranty and follow-up details.
   - Strategic value: high commercial intent but price-sensitive.

4. **Mobility-limited patient or caregiver**
   - Need: home-visit suitability, family coordination, clear boundaries about what requires a clinic.
   - Strategic value: differentiated local service if the operating model is genuinely available.

## Offer ladder

Only activate an offer after completing `docs/business-facts-checklist.md`.

1. **Low-friction entry:** phone/WhatsApp guidance and appointment selection.
2. **Assessment:** appropriate clinic hearing evaluation with a clear result discussion.
3. **Solution:** device shortlist and written comparison based on hearing and lifestyle needs.
4. **Delivery:** fitting, orientation, care instructions, and documented warranty.
5. **Retention:** scheduled adjustments, cleaning, maintenance, consumables, repairs, and annual review reminders where clinically appropriate.
6. **Optional recurring revenue:** verified rental and care plans with signed terms and operational capacity.

## 12-week execution plan

### Weeks 1–2: facts, tracking, and local entity

- Complete every applicable item in the business-facts checklist.
- Replace generic clinician profiles with verified named professionals.
- Verify the exact NAP everywhere: website, Google Business Profile, Bing Places, Apple Business Connect, Justdial, social profiles, brand listings, and clinic signage.
- Configure GA4 conversions for `click_call`, `click_whatsapp`, `click_directions`, `book_appointment_click`, `cta_form_start`, `contact_form_submit`, and `generate_lead`.
- In Google Search Console, submit `https://audiosen.com/sitemap.xml` and inspect the key service URLs.
- Record the starting metrics before judging growth.

### Weeks 3–4: conversion and trust assets

- Photograph the exterior/signage, route from landmark, reception, testing space, equipment, and real team.
- Publish verified clinician pages with registration and schedule details.
- Put the direct Google review link into a QR card and WhatsApp follow-up template.
- Ask every eligible real patient for an honest review; never reward, script, gate, or pressure reviews.
- Create a written quote template that clearly separates device, accessories, professional services, warranty, taxes, and follow-up.
- Test every phone, WhatsApp, map, form, and confirmation-email path on mobile.

### Weeks 5–8: search demand and referral engine

- Publish one accurate, clinician-reviewed article each week.
- Add one genuine Google Business Profile update and new real photographs each week.
- Request listings/links from real manufacturers, professional associations, partner clinics, hospitals, senior communities, schools, employers, and local organizations.
- Build a referral note and closed-loop update process for ENT doctors and physicians, subject to patient consent.
- Create short educational Hindi/English videos answering actual questions received by the clinic.

### Weeks 9–12: controlled acquisition test

- Launch a small Google Search campaign only after conversion events work.
- Limit location to the practical Dehradun service area; do not target all India initially.
- Start with exact/phrase commercial intent: hearing test Dehradun, hearing aid centre Dehradun, hearing aid repair Dehradun, and audiologist Dehradun only when clinician facts are live.
- Add negatives such as jobs, course, training, salary, PDF, free download, wholesale, used, and DIY.
- Send each ad group to its matching service page, not the home page.
- Review actual search terms and qualified leads weekly. Pause keywords that spend without producing relevant enquiries.

## Weekly founder dashboard

Track one row per week:

| Metric | Source | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Non-brand search impressions/clicks | Search Console | Early organic demand |
| Top queries and landing pages | Search Console | Content and page priorities |
| Calls, WhatsApp, forms, directions | GA4 | Lead volume by action |
| Qualified appointments | Clinic CRM/sheet | Real pipeline, not vanity traffic |
| Show rate | Clinic CRM/sheet | Scheduling quality |
| Assessment-to-device conversion | Clinic CRM/sheet | Sales effectiveness |
| Average order value and gross margin | Accounts | Sustainable economics |
| Follow-up/repair revenue | Accounts | Retention and lifetime value |
| Google reviews and average rating | Business Profile | Local trust/prominence |
| Advertising cost per qualified lead | Google Ads + CRM | Paid-channel viability |

Do not optimize for page views alone. The core business metric is contribution margin from satisfied, appropriately served patients after acquisition, fitting, warranty, and aftercare costs.

## Operating cadence

- **Daily:** answer calls/forms/WhatsApp promptly; record source and outcome for every enquiry.
- **Monday:** review missed leads, appointments, repairs, and previous-week metrics.
- **Wednesday:** publish or update one useful piece of content or FAQ based on real questions.
- **Friday:** request eligible reviews, upload current photos, reply to all reviews, and check profile accuracy.
- **Monthly:** compare query/page performance, update weak pages, audit NAP, review complaints/returns, and decide one experiment for the next month.

## Guardrails

- No fake reviews, review incentives, purchased links, copied articles, doorway pages, invented credentials, or “guaranteed cure/best” claims.
- No patient story or clinical record without explicit permission.
- No ENT-specialist positioning without a real registered ENT professional.
- No price, discount, trial, warranty, or same-day promise without documented terms and delivery capacity.
- Treat hearing aids as regulated medical devices and confirm current sale/distribution requirements with a qualified adviser.
